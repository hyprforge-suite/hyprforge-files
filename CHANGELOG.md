# Changelog

This component is released with the Hyprforge suite and shares its version: see the suite's `CHANGELOG.md`.

## [0.1.9] - 2026-10-08

- Phones and cameras in Devices
- Quick Look plays videos and turns 3D models
- Previous Versions, from snapper's snapshots
- One Files window: folders from other apps open as tabs, on Hyprland
- Tags, kept on the file the way Dolphin keeps them
- drag_show: doc links rustdoc can resolve
- Notifications say who did what, from where to where, in how long
- A drag you can see, messages as notifications, and a way out of admin
- Open as administrator, through a pkexec helper
- Drags inside the window stay in it, and folders spring open
- Free space, an undo history, and Typing in a folder in Preferences
